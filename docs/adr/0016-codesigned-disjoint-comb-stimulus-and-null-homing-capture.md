# ADR-0016: Co-Designed Disjoint-Comb Stimulus, Closed-Loop Feedback-Adaptive Capture, Acoustic Null Homing & Live Null-Test Auto-Patch

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

Hardware re-amping, multi-control manifold mapping, and wet/dry alignment across multi-regime musical rigs face five coupled signal-processing hazards (`REQ-1.6`, `REQ-1.7`, `REQ-1.8`, `REQ-2.1`, `REQ-2.3`):

1. **Why Static 30-Second Sweeps & Arbitrary Musical Stems Fail (`Theorem 3`, `Theorems 4.1–4.8`):** Uncoordinated log-sine sweeps, pink noise, and arbitrary musical stems suffer from broadband intermodulation collision (even $2f$ and odd $3f$ products folding directly onto linear modal frequency bins), blind open-loop excitation that fails to find hardware noise-gate/compressor thresholds or high-$Q$ cabinet resonances, and uncalibrated converter/microphone/preamp front-end headroom.
2. **All-Pass Group-Delay Bias & Multi-Regime Latency Drift (`Theorems 4.1–4.3`):** Phase-dispersive analog preamps, wah inductors, and cabinet crossovers shift the peak of raw Generalized Cross-Correlation (`GCC-PHAT`) away from the true causal wave-front onset by up to `1.5–8.0` samples, while naive single-window FFT division $\hat{H}[k] = Y[k]/X[k]$ amplifies quiescent noise in spectral notches and conflates free-running LFO, echo tails, or mains hum with input-driven distortion.
3. **High-Dimensional Control Combinatorics, Mode-0 Baseline Drift & Potentiometer Backlash (`Theorem 3`, `Theorem 3.5`):** Exhaustive multi-knob grid captures scale exponentially with control count $P$, unanchored bases corrupt the `StarCenter` reference when `default_val` is asymmetric, and physical potentiometer detents suffer from mechanical backlash unless guided by live acoustic null homing (`< 0.05 dB`).
4. **Closed-Loop Noise Bias & Upstream Analog Front-End Clipping (`Theorem 4.4`, `Laws 0.A–0.F`):** Naively adapting stimulus amplitudes inside a single window introduces closed-loop correlation with thermal noise, while sub-`0 dBFS` microphone capsule (`X_max`) or external mic preamp clipping upstream of the ADC goes undetected by digital peak meters.
5. **Post-Fit Residual Drift Before Final Export (`Theorem 4.4`, `Theorem 4.8`):** Without an immediate closed-loop hardware verification pass at the end of capture, subtle localized spectral or dynamic discrepancies cannot be corrected while the hardware rig is still at `StarCenter`.

## 3. Decision & Mathematical Formulation

We adopt **Candidate C** across `packages/capture` and `packages/studio` ([`../specs/training/capture_and_alignment.md`](../specs/training/capture_and_alignment.md), [`../specs/architecture/psychoacoustic_quality_and_tiers.md`](../specs/architecture/psychoacoustic_quality_and_tiers.md), [`../specs/dsp/parametric_control_bus.md`](../specs/dsp/parametric_control_bus.md), `ref[train.capture.invariants]`, `ref[train.capture.planner.schedule]`, `ref[train.capture.alignment.multi-regime]`):

1. **`Theorem 3` & `Theorem 3.5` — `StarCenter` Mode-0 Lock, Triangular Newton `CP1` Basis for Arbitrary $P$, Mode A/B Control Capture & Automatic 1D Commutator Interaction Pruning (`ref[train.capture.planner.schedule]`, `ref[dsp.ctrl.basis.lobatto]`, `ref[dsp.ctrl.switch.option-contributors]`):**
   - **`Theorem 3` (`StarCenter` Mode-0 Lock & Triangular Newton Basis for `CP1` Contributors Across Arbitrary $P$ Controls):** Anchors `ControlClampDescriptor::eval_lobatto_basis` at $a_{p,1} = u_{\text{default},p}$, $a_{p,2} = -1.0$, $a_{p,3} = +1.0$ via the triangular Newton basis $\psi_{p,1}(u_p) = u_p - a_{p,1}$, $\psi_{p,2}(u_p) = (u_p - a_{p,1})(u_p + 1)$, $\psi_{p,3}(u_p) = (u_p - a_{p,1})(u_p + 1)(u_p - 1)$ so $\boldsymbol{\psi}(\mathbf{a}_1) = \mathbf{0}$ identically at `StarCenter` even for asymmetric defaults, and represents each non-reference discrete `Switch` option $o \neq o_{\text{def}}$ (`def_opt`) as a `k = 1` contributor $\psi_{q,o}(\sigma_q) = \mathbf{1}(\sigma_q = o)$ (`0.0` at `StarCenter`), locking $\mathbf{W}_{\text{head}}^{(0)} \equiv \mathbf{W}^{\star}(\mathbf{u}_{\text{StarCenter}})$ to `0.000000 ULP` across arbitrary $P \in [0, 65535]$ controls.
   - **Instant $\Delta T_{\text{mem}}(p)$ Mode A/B Classification, `< 0.05 dB` Live Acoustic Null Homing & `<= 0.15 dB` Thermal/Sag Recovery Gate:** At the `Min` (`-1`) endpoint (`0.5s` homing stop), classifies each control $p$ by memory-tail shift $\Delta T_{\text{mem}}(p)$: **`Mode A` (`3.5s` continuous `Min -> Max` dynamic sweep)** when $\Delta T_{\text{mem}}(p) \le 15\text{ ms}$, or **`Mode B` (`1.0s` adaptive stationary stops)** when $\Delta T_{\text{mem}}(p) > 15\text{ ms}$ (or when pot crackle / user `"Prefer Stationary Stops"` override is active), guided by **`< 0.05 dB` live acoustic null homing** for physical knob positioning and a **`<= 0.15 dB` thermal/sag recovery gate** when returning to `StarCenter`.
   - **`Theorem 3.5` (Jacobian Sweet-Spot Velocity Tracking & Automatic 1D Commutator Interaction Pruning + `2` Orthogonal `3.0s` Hadamard Diagonals):** Tracks local `EMR`-weighted acoustic Jacobian velocity $J_p[r]$ across the $35$ `100 ms` windows of each Mode A sweep to weight cubic `CP1` projections around circuit bifurcations, tests 1D commutator/spectral-shape separability to prune uncoupled controls ($\mathbf{v}_p = \mathbf{0}$), and excites coupled controls using at most **`2` orthogonal `3.0s` Hadamard diagonals** (skipping both diagonals in `0.0s` when $P_{\text{coupled}} \le 1$).

2. **`Theorems 4.1–4.3` — Disjoint-Comb Forward Pedestal-Sideband Ratio, Reference-Anchor Multi-Frame Pooling $\Omega_{\text{ref}}$ & `500 ms` `A5` Musical Transient Coreset (`ref[train.capture.invariants]`, `ref[train.capture.alignment.multi-regime]`, `ref[qual.besr.noise-subtracted]`):**
   - Synthesizes `96.0 ms` cyclic frames (`4,608` samples at `48 kHz` = `512`-sample `10.67 ms` cyclic prefix with `64`-sample $C^1$ Hann carrier crossfade + `4,096`-sample `85.33 ms` analysis window, $\Delta f = 11.71875\text{ Hz}$) using an integer-cycle **`93.75 Hz` pedestal ($k_p = 2P = 8$**, completing `1` exact cycle in the `512`-sample prefix and `9` cycles per frame) and odd multisine carriers $\Omega_{\text{in}} \subset (2\mathbb{Z}+1)\omega_0$ with spacing **$k_{i+1} - k_i \ge 8P + 2 = 34$ bins**. Linear carriers ($k_i$), asymmetric $l = 1$ intermodulation sidebands ($k_i \pm 8$), symmetric $l = 2$ intermodulation sidebands ($k_i \pm 16$), and unexcited guard bins ($\Omega_{\text{guard}}$) are strictly disjoint.
   - Normalizes per-frame sideband ratios $R_l^{(m)}(\omega_k)$ across $\ge 4$ fixed reference anchors $\Omega_{\text{ref}} \subset \Omega_{\text{in}}^{(m)}$ to cancel frame-varying drive/gain factors $\ln \alpha_l^{(m)}$ before inverse-variance pooling, pairs with two-phase coarse-to-fine sub-sample alignment ($\le 0.01\text{ sample}$ via `64`-tap Kaiser-sinc fractional realignment), and embeds a deterministic **`500 ms` `A5` Musical Transient Coreset** for any musical source.

3. **`Theorem 4.4` — Martingale-Unbiased Real-Time Frame-Recursive Feedback Excitation (`AdaptiveStimulusController`), Pre-Synthesis Safeguard `Laws 0.A–0.F` & Adaptation `Laws 1–3`:**
   - Because each frame's waveform $x^{(m)}[n]$ is synthesized deterministically from $\mathcal{F}_{m-1}$-measurable statistics of frames $\le m - 1$, the martingale difference identity $\mathbb{E}[\boldsymbol{\phi}(x^{(m)}[n])v^{(m)}[n] \mid \mathcal{F}_{m-1}] = \mathbf{0}$ guarantees **zero closed-loop noise bias** (`< 1.5 ms` compute budget per `96 ms` frame).
   - **Pre-Synthesis Hardware Safeguard `Laws 0.A–0.F`:**
     - **`Law 0.A` (Autonomous LFO Bessel-Sideband Stroboscopic Averaging & 2-Level `-18 / -3 dBFS` Envelope-SVF Pole-Zoom):** Detects inter-frame LTV drift ($\rho_{\text{LTV}}^{(1)} > 0.02$) or adjacent even-bin Bessel sidebands ($\eta_{\text{Bessel}}^{(m)} > 10^{-3}$) to replace $\lvert\hat{T}_{\text{carrier}}^{(m)}(\omega_k)\rvert$ with its LFO-cycle stroboscopic RMS average $\bar{T}_{\text{LFO}}(\omega_k)$, and runs 2-level (`-18 dBFS` and `-3 dBFS`) Pole-Zoom on envelope-tracked SVFs/auto-wahs.
     - **`Law 0.B` (Causal Known-History Echo Deflation & 2-Frame `192 ms` Periodic Hold):** Subtracts the online `TORA` Stage-IV tank/echo prediction driven by known past inputs $x^{(0..m-1)}[n]$ before FFT evaluation and holds spectral updates for a 2-frame pair (`192 ms`, `106.67 ms` periodic prefix) whenever $\hat{T}_{\text{mem}} > 9.33\text{ ms}$.
     - **`Law 0.C` (Mains-Hum Comb Exclusion, Idle-Squeal Kurtosis Mask & `96 ms` USB Dropout Single-Frame Replay):** Excludes `50/60 Hz` harmonic bins $\Omega_{\text{mains}}$, masks autonomous parasitic squeal bins ($\kappa_{\text{spec}}(\omega) > 10$ on $\Omega_{\text{guard}}$) from $\Omega_{\text{zoom}}$ and `EMR`, and discards/replays any single `96 ms` frame suffering a USB/ASIO clock slip ($\lvert\Delta\hat{\tau}_0^{(m)}\rvert > 0.25\text{ samp}$) without restarting the stem.
     - **`Law 0.D` (Predictive `-1.5 dBFS` / `0.841 FS` ADC Peak Governor & Reference-Anchor Thermal Drift Tracker):** Constrains predicted wet peaks via IFFT crest-factor lookahead to $\le -1.5\text{ dBFS}$ (`0.841 FS`) and tracks reference-anchor sideband means $\hat{\alpha}_{1,2}^{(m)}$ on $\Omega_{\text{ref}}$ to de-trend slow tube/Germanium thermal bias drift frame-by-frame.
     - **`Law 0.E` (Sub-Harmonic Chatter Cap & Glissando Handoff):** Detects sub-harmonic flip-flop divider chatter ($\omega < \min(\Omega_{\text{in}})$ or $k_i/2$ with $\gamma_{xy}^2 < 0.7$) in Frames 0–1, capping polyphonic multisine excitation at `2` frames (`192 ms`) and handing off immediately to the `250 ms` Monophonic Glissando (`A3`) + `500 ms` Coreset (`A5`).
     - **`Law 0.F` (Sub-`0 dBFS` Microphone Capsule / Preamp Clipping & LF Excursion `X_max` Governor):** Detects upstream mic FET/preamp clipping and `60–120 Hz` speaker/capsule excursion bottoming well below `0 dBFS` via post-cabinet `8–20 kHz` stopband harmonic skirt excess ($\Gamma_{\text{post-cab}}^{(m)} > 10.0$), un-dispersed sub-`0 dBFS` flat-rail plateau concentration ($\kappa_{\text{rail}}^{(m)}$), and super-cubic LF intermodulation ($\Delta\text{THD}/\Delta L > 6\text{ dB/dB}$). Applies non-stop soft recovery (clamping boost-induced overload to `0.80 A_rail`, or extrapolating `Master Volume` output-stage overload from unclipped `-18 / -6 dB` `Law 4C` sub-frames and dual-mic cross-handoff) while surfacing a one-click stem re-capture workflow backed by a **`400 ms` `-18 dB` differential mic-pad reference probe**.
   - **Closed-Loop Adaptation `Laws 1–3`:**
     - **`Law 1` (3-Branch `Gated` / `Pure-LTI` / `Nonlinear` Gate + Bounded `±12 dB` Water-Filling Inverse-Pre-Emphasis):** Steps amplitude up to open noise gates (`Branch 1A`), classifies pure LTI systems without `0/0` sideband division (`Branch 1B`), or applies bounded `[0.25, 4.0]` (`±12 dB`) inverse-pre-emphasis water-filling at constant predicted Stage-I output power $P_{v_1}^{\star}$ (`Branch 1C`).
     - **`Law 2` (Dual-Regime Sub-Hz Pole-Zoom Spectroscopy & Schroeder Phase Re-Phasing):** Zooms on up to $K_{\text{zoom}} \le 8$ high-$Q$ resonances/notches using `Regime 2A` (`Pedestal-Off` adjacent odd bins, $\Delta f = 23.44\text{ Hz}$) or `Regime 2B` (`Pedestal-On` staggered multi-frame interleaved comb $+2r\omega_0$ with 2-frame `192 ms` hold when $\hat{r}_q > 0.990$), recomputing Schroeder phases in `< 5 µs` (crest factor $\le 4.5\text{ dB}$).
     - **`Law 3` (Simultaneous Psychoacoustic `EMR` + `Theorem 4.7` Block-$D$-Optimal `256-D` Active Learning + `Theorem 4.8` Two-Episode "Pump-and-Probe" Bloom + 4-Gate Cramér-Rao Stopping):** Weights carriers by 33-band Bark/ERB Audible Error-to-Mask Ratio (`EMR`), selects from **5 operator-aligned phase-space trajectory primitives (`Theorem 4.7`: $\mathcal{P}_1$ `T1SubHarmonicTracker` branch-cut chirp, $\mathcal{P}_2$ `Stage II` multi-drive rail-crossing & $C^2$ wavefolder extrema, $\mathcal{P}_3$ `QuadratureHysteresis2D` constant-flux LF sweep, $\mathcal{P}_4$ `Stage IV` tank knee & ducking-release burst, and $\mathcal{P}_5$ `Theorem 4.8` Two-Episode "Pump-and-Probe" ballistic bloom waveform with reverse-biased `512`-sample sub-threshold periodic comb probe)** to maximize Cholesky log-determinant information gain across the 7 intra-tier `256-D` feature blocks, and terminates stationary stems in **`0.54s–3.5s`** (`T_max = 6.0s` when Episode 2 triggers, or optional `--fixed-duration`) as soon as all **4 Cramér-Rao convergence gates** lock.

4. **`Theorem 4.5`, `Theorem 4.6` & Automatic `1.0s` Live Hardware-vs-Model Null-Test (`< 5 ms` Auto-Patch):**
   - **`Theorem 4.5` (Multi-Port / Stereo / Aux-Key / `4CM` / `WDW` Orthogonal Excitation):** Excites `2 -> 2` stereo via half-stride-interleaved disjoint combs ($k_{p,L} = 8$, $k_{p,R} = 16$) + a `288 ms` 3-State (`Pure Mid`, `Pure Side`, `Hard-Pan + Quiet Witness`) spatial parity & stereo-link probe; disambiguates `2 -> 1` dual-input sum vs. external aux-key sidechain in Frame 0 (`96 ms`); and runs parallel orthogonal `AdaptiveStimulusController` loops across `4CM` (`FX Send` + `FX Return`) and `WDW` ports simultaneously in wall-clock time.
   - **`Theorem 4.6` (Intra-Sweep Adaptation `Laws 4A–4D`):** During `Mode A` (`3.5s`) and Hadamard (`3.0s`) sweeps, executes `Law 4A` (online Jacobian-sensitivity water-filling for `Class I/IV` tone/level knobs), `Law 4B` (kinematic $\alpha$-$\beta$ pole-velocity lookahead tracker for `Class II` swept wah/resonant peaks), `Law 4C` (intra-frame `3 x 32 ms` `{-18, -6, 0 dB}` micro-dynamic staircase for `Class III` drive/comp/gate sweeps), and **`Law 4D` (`1.2s` / `12`-frame early stopping on `3.0s` Hadamard diagonal sweeps when the cross-interaction residual $S_{\text{FM}}^{(r)}$ is below $10^{-4}$ for 4 consecutive frames)**.
   - **Automatic `1.0s` Live Hardware-vs-Model Null-Test & `< 5 ms` Auto-Patch:** At the end of every live hardware capture while the rig remains at `StarCenter`, streams a `1.0s` `EMR`-weighted out-of-sample verification burst through both hardware and `DnamEngine` simultaneously; if any band exceeds threshold, applies a **`< 5 ms` rank-1 `MM-KKT` auto-patch** to `Arena 4` ($\mathbf{G}_0, \mathbf{c}_{0,\text{eff}}^{\text{MM-KKT}}$), re-solves the `Theorem 2` intra-tier `64-D` Schur cascade + `Theorem 6` zero-DC contraction, and hot-swaps via `DnamEngine::reload_model_in_place` with zero heap allocations.
